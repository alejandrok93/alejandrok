Legal Tech AI/ML Production

QualifyAI — Intelligent Lead Qualification

Built an AI-powered lead scoring system with explainable confidence levels, automated workflow triggers, and reporting dashboards for legal intake teams.

Business Problem

Law firms receive hundreds of inbound leads but lack a systematic way to qualify them. Manual review is inconsistent, slow, and leads to missed opportunities or wasted attorney time on low-quality prospects.

Technical Challenge

Build a scoring system that attorneys actually trust. The model needed to produce explainable scores (not black-box predictions), handle variable data quality from different intake sources, and integrate seamlessly into existing CRM workflows.

Constraints

  • Explainability required — attorneys need to understand why a lead scored high or low
  • Must integrate with existing CRM workflows without disruption
  • Data privacy compliance for sensitive client information
  • Handle inconsistent lead data quality across intake channels

What I Owned

Led architecture design and full-stack implementation. Owned the scoring algorithm design, confidence calibration, workflow trigger system, reporting dashboard, and cross-team coordination with product and customer success.

Approach

  • Designed multi-signal scoring with weighted factors and transparent reasoning
  • Built confidence calibration layer that surfaces uncertainty to users
  • Created configurable workflow triggers based on score thresholds
  • Implemented reporting dashboard for intake team visibility and audit trails
  • Shipped iteratively with tight feedback loops from early-access firms

Outcomes

  • Reduced manual lead review time for intake teams
  • Improved intake consistency across participating firms
  • Automated workflow routing based on qualification scores
  • Shipped to production with measurable adoption and retention
Stack: Ruby on Rails, React, LLMs, PostgreSQL, ElasticSearch
Legal Tech Generative AI Full-Stack

AI-Powered Email Generation for Law Firms

Designed and shipped an LLM-powered email template generator that uses firm and contact context to produce personalized client communications.

Business Problem

Attorneys spend significant time writing personalized outreach and follow-up emails. Template libraries help, but they're generic and don't account for specific firm tone, practice area, or contact context.

Technical Challenge

Integrate LLM capabilities into a production CRM where output quality, latency, and cost all matter. Generated emails must match firm voice, use accurate contact details, and never hallucinate legal information.

Constraints

  • Output must be professional and legally appropriate
  • Must use real firm and contact data without leaking across tenants
  • Latency budget: users expect near-instant generation
  • Cost-effective at scale across thousands of firms

What I Owned

End-to-end feature ownership: prompt engineering, API integration, context assembly pipeline, UI implementation, and production rollout.

Approach

  • Built context assembly pipeline that pulls firm profile, contact history, and practice area data
  • Engineered prompts with structured output constraints and tone calibration
  • Implemented streaming responses for perceived performance
  • Added guardrails to prevent hallucinated legal claims or incorrect contact details

Outcomes

  • Enabled contextual email drafting for thousands of law firms
  • Reduced email composition time for attorneys
  • Shipped with high adoption rates across the customer base
Stack: Ruby on Rails, React, LLM APIs, PostgreSQL
